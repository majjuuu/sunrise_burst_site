About the research

We are developing a website to help inspect solar radio observations from e-Callisto stations. The planned workflow accepts FIT files, processes observations in 15-minute windows, and uses a YOLOv8 model to locate and classify candidate radio bursts.

This site is currently a front-end demo. The spectrogram and detection shown on the Detector page are synthetic examples; uploaded files are not analyzed until a backend is connected.

Team

Erica Ma and Praneel work on SunRISE software, detection, and classification. This demo is intended for Marquette students working with e-Callisto observations.

What comes next

  • Connect the upload form to the detection backend.
  • Display model results with burst type, time, frequency range, and confidence.
  • Replace the synthetic sample with observations and outputs that the team has reviewed for public use.

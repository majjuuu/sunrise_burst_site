How Detection and Classification Work

Planned research workflow; this demo currently shows synthetic results.

  1. Collect solar radio observations as e-Callisto FIT files.
  2. Preprocess the spectrograms into tiles for model input.
  3. Detect candidate bursts and their bounding boxes with YOLOv8.
  4. Classify burst types with a separate model planned for future work.
  5. Review each result's time, frequency range, and confidence.
